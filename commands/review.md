Load `paper-reviewer`. Produce three independent reports:

1. **Reviewer 1 — sympathetic expert**: is the advance real and correctly

2. **Reviewer 2 — hostile expert**: find the weakest inference, the missing
   control, the overclaim, the unfair comparison.
3. **Editor**: is this in scope, is the significance sufficient, what would
   trigger desk rejection?

Then produce a consolidated Major / Minor / Editorial list. Every criticism
must point to a specific section, paragraph, figure, or table. Do not invent
reviewer comments that the manuscript does not earn.
