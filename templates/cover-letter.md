# Cover Letter 模板

> 规则：一页；不重复摘要；说清"为什么是这本刊"；保密信息单独标出。
> Nature 正刊的 cover letter 是可选的；Cell/Science 通常建议写。

---

Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript **“<Title>”** for publication in *<Journal>* as a
<Article / Report / Research Article>.

**What is new.** <One sentence: the single most important advance. No hype.>

**Why it matters to your readers.** <Two or three sentences: the broad problem,
what was previously impossible, and what this enables. Write for a
non-specialist editor.>

**Why this journal.** <One or two sentences connecting the advance to the
journal's scope and recent papers. Be specific; do not flatter.>

**Evidence.** <One sentence on the strength of the evidence: independent
validation, sample size, replication, causal design.>

We confirm that this work is original, is not under consideration elsewhere,
and has been approved by all authors. <If applicable: a preprint is available at
<DOI>; we have followed the journal's preprint policy.>

<Confidential note, if any: related work in press, conflicts, prior discussions
with the editor.>

Sincerely,

<Corresponding author>
<Affiliation>
<Email> · <ORCID>
